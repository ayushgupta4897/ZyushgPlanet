## 0. The ladder, and what "credible" actually means

| Level | Operational meaning | Who decides | What it takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TCE / signal** | A periodic dip above a detection threshold. Kepler TPS and TESS SPOC: MES > 7.1 with ≥ 2 transits (TESS) / ≥ 3 (Kepler) and passing transit-consistency tests; QLP: "signal-to-pink-noise ratio > 9 and BLS peak significance > 9" **[verified: Guerrero et al. 2021, arXiv:2103.12538 §V; Thompson et al. 2018, arXiv:1710.06758]** | Your pipeline | A search |
| **Planet candidate (PC)** | Passes every not-transit-like, stellar-eclipse, centroid-offset and ephemeris-match test; novelty checked; no formal FPP required. Kepler KOI "CANDIDATE", TESS "PC". | Robovetter / TOI team / you | Sections 1-5 |
| **Statistically validated (VP)** | Astrophysical FPP below a published threshold: vespa FPP < 1 % (Morton et al. 2016: 1,935 KOIs, 1,284 newly validated **[verified abstract]**); TRICERATOPS FPP < 0.015 **and** NFPP < 10⁻³ (Giacalone et al. 2021 **[verified abstract]**); Giacalone et al. 2022 used the stricter FPP < 0.01, NFPP < 10⁻³, 20 runs averaged **[verified §III]**, normally *with* high-resolution imaging (companions to ~5") and reconnaissance spectroscopy, published in a refereed journal. SG1 code **VP** = "statistically validated planet that does not have a mass measurement" **[verified: SG1 Guidelines rev 6.4, 2020-09-04]** | Referees + the NASA Exoplanet Archive, which lists it as "Published Confirmed" | Section 6 |
| **Confirmed (CP / P)** | Mass measured (RV, TTV). SG1 code **P** = "confirmed planet with a mass measurement"; TFOPWG disposition **CP**. | Referees + Archive | Section 7 |

## 1. The Kepler Robovetter: the canonical test battery (Thompson et al. 2018 DR25; Coughlin et al. 2016 DR24; Coughlin et al. 2014)

Decision tree (Thompson 2018 Fig. 4): (a) secondary of an earlier TCE on the same star? (b) transit-like (NT)? (c) significant secondary (SS)? (d) in parallel, centroid offset (CO) and ephemeris match (EC). The four major flags are exported as `koi_fpflag_nt/ss/co/ec` **[verified: API_kepcandidate_columns.html]**: NT = "light curve is not consistent with that of a transiting planet"; SS = "significant secondary event, transit shape, or out-of-eclipse variability"; CO = "source of the signal is from a nearby star, as inferred by measuring the centroid location" (also set when the signal is stronger in halo than core pixels); EC = "shares the same period and epoch as another object and is judged to be the result of flux contamination". Every test runs on two detrendings (DV and ALT); failure on either counts. **Table 4 of Thompson 2018 ships three threshold sets — DR25, "High C" and "High R" [run: thompson2018.txt l.1254-1270]**; the High-R (reliability) column is the conservative choice for a small candidate hunt.

### 1.1 Not-transit-like (NT) tests and thresholds **[run: Thompson 2018 PDF text]**

| Test | Definition | DR25 threshold |
|---|---|---|

### 1.3 Centroid-offset (CO) tests **[run: Thompson App. A.5; Twicken 2018 PDF; Coughlin 2016 PDF]**

- Needs ≥ 3 quarterly difference images with a discernible stellar image; otherwise CENT_FEW_DIFFS / CENT_FEW_MEAS, no decision.
- **Resolved**: brightest difference-image pixel > 1.5 px (≈ 6") from the target in ≥ 2/3 of quarters → CENT_RESOLVED_OFFSET.
- **Unresolved**: PRF-fit difference-image centroid offset from the out-of-transit PRF centroid (or KIC position) significant at **3σ and > 2", or 4σ and > 1"** → CENT_UNRESOLVED_OFFSET. DV adds a quadrature error term 0.2/3 = 0.0667" so "the minimum 3σ uncertainty in the magnitude of the mean offset is therefore 0.2 arcsec, and no offset less than that is considered significant"; quarterly offsets are "robustly averaged"; Kepler DV "does not account for proper motion in catalog coordinates" (Twicken et al. 2018, arXiv:1803.04526).
- Informational flags: CENT_KIC_POS, CENT_CROWDED, CENT_SATURATED, CENT_INVERT_DIFF (star *brightens* in transit), CENT_UNCERTAIN, HALO_GHOST (ghost diagnostic: halo-aperture correlation exceeds core's; 9.7 % of DR25 TCEs carried HALO_GHOST **[run l.3781]**).
- Calibration **[verified Coughlin 2016 §4]**: off-target injections gave "a ∼50% detection rate at a separation of 2" (0.5 pixels) when recovered with a MES of 20 (Mullally et al. 2015a)". **This is the sensitivity floor of pixel vetting at Kepler's 3.98" pixels.**
- Archive columns **[verified]**: `koi_dicco_msky` (difference-image PRF centroid vs out-of-transit centroid, arcsec), `koi_dikco_msky` (vs KIC position), `koi_fwm_stat_sig` (flux-weighted offset significance in %, 100 % = no offset), `koi_comment` (minor flags).

### 1.4 Ephemeris match (EC) **[verified: Coughlin et al. 2014 arXiv:1401.1240 HTML; Thompson 2018 App. A.6]**

Coughlin 2014: ΔP = (P_A − P_B)/P_A, ΔT = (T_A − T_B)/P_A; ΔP' = |ΔP − round(ΔP)|, ΔT' = |ΔT − round(ΔT)|; σ = √2·erfcinv(Δ'). Match requires **σ_P ≥ 3.5 and σ_T ≥ 2.0**, checked A→B and B→A. (0.674, 0.674) is "the worst match possible" (Δ' = 0.5), where random pairs cluster; real matches centre near (4.5, 3.0). Any integer ratio is allowed (77 % of FP KOIs 1:1, 19 % 1:2, 4 % odd ratios). Spatial criteria (one of): (a) d < d_max = 50·√(10⁶·10^(−0.4 m_kep) + 1)" with the brighter star's Kp; (b) antipodal about the FOV centre within 50" (12.5 px); (c) same CCD module within 10 px of the same row or column in any of 4 quarters. Mechanisms: direct PRF (incl. field-flattener ghosts), antipodal reflection, CCD crosstalk, column anomaly. Depth model D2/D1 = 10^(−0.4Δm)/f_prf(d) + 1, f_prf = ½exp(−d²/α²) + ½γ²/(d²+γ²), α = 6.73", γ = 0.406"; 685 KOIs (12 %) FPs from 409 parents, 118 found by no other method, 31 "bastards".

DR25 variant: period and epoch differences in units of the injection-measured DV ephemeris uncertainty (S_P, S_T; perfect match 0); match if **S_P < 5 and S_T < 5 with period ratio < 50**, different KIC IDs, and one of: d < 55·√(10⁶·10^(−0.4 m_kep)+1)"; antipodal within 100" (25 px); same module within 5 px in column; same module within 5 px in row and column. Compared against all DR25 TCEs, KOIs, EBWG EBs, Kreiner ground EBs, TrES EBs and the GCVS.

### 1.5 Disposition score and code

`koi_score` = fraction of 10,000 Monte-Carlo re-runs returning PC; "confidence in the KOI disposition", not a planet probability **[verified column doc]**. Public C++: `g++ -std=c++11 -O2 -o robovet Kepler-RoboVetter-DR25.cpp`; `./robovet INPUTFILE OUTFILE NMC` with shipped OBS/INV/SCR1-3/INJ1-3 inputs; ≥ 100 iterations "for useable scores, and preferably at least 1,000" **[verified README, https://github.com/nasa/kepler-robovetter]**. Borrow: the NT → SS → CO → EC structure, model-shift statistics (reimplemented in LEO-Vetter and exovetter), individual-transit culling (Rubble/Chases/Zuma/Marshall), occultation exceptions, and **inverted (INV) and scrambled (SCR) runs to calibrate the vetter's false-alarm rate** — exactly Kuber's and eumemic's inverted-flux controls.

## 2. TESS vetting ecosystem and the TOI/TFOP standards

### 2.1 How a TOI is made **[verified: Guerrero et al. 2021 HTML]**

- SPOC: TCE if MES > 7.1, ≥ 2 transits, transit-consistency tests; DV runs odd/even, weak secondary (albedo/T_eff of the "planet"), ghost diagnostic, difference-image PRF centroids (quadrature term chosen so offsets ≪ 7.5" are *not* 3σ-significant), flux-weighted centroid motion, bootstrap FAP. QLP: BLS; TCE if signal-to-pink-noise > 9 and BLS significance > 9; triage Tmag ≤ 10.5 (prime mission), R_p > 35 R_E removed, 6 % depth cut for stars without radii (Teff > 3500 K, or < 4000 K with PM < 10 mas/yr).
- Triage: TEC (https://github.com/christopherburke/TESS-ExoClass; Burke, Coughlin, Mullally, Garcia Soto, Mullally, Christiansen) Tier 1 (no flags), Tier 2 (≥ 1 warning), Tier 3 (significant secondary / coherent variability — mostly EBs); only Tiers 1-2 go to vetters.
- Group vetting: "a team of at least three vetters visually inspect each TCE", ~50 TCEs per 2-h session, up to four sessions per week; promotes to TOI, sets TFOP priorities. TOI list "updated automatically (twice daily) on ExoFOP-TESS"; MAST archives a monthly static copy.
- TFOPWG dispositions `tfopwg_disp` ∈ {APC ambiguous planetary candidate, CP, FA, FP, KP, PC}; `pl_tranmid` in BJD; `pl_trandep` in ppm **[verified API_TOI_columns.html]**.
- CTOIs: "ExoFOP emphasizes CTOIs should only be reported if there is no TOI with the same period"; 169 prime-mission CTOIs were promoted, 106 "recovered from TCEs mistakenly ruled out in pre-vetting triage"; CTOIs with no TCE and no QLP/SPOC products "would need to be recovered manually in a reprocessing step".
- Multiplicity boost: "(75/930)/(930/232705) ≈ 20" for all SPOC candidates; "(69/545)/(545/232705) ≈ 54" for < 6 R_E; "We advise caution when applying the multiplicity boost to candidate multi-transiting systems in crowded regions".
- **Has any pipeline triggered on this TESS star?** The Exoplanet Archive has no TESS TCE table. Use the MAST bulk CSVs **[run]**: https://archive.stsci.edu/missions/tess/catalogs/tce/ (`tess<timestamp>-s<start>-s<stop>_dvr-tcestats.csv`, single sectors 1-107 plus multi-sector bundles) with columns `tceid, ticid, tce_plnt_num, sectors, tce_period, tce_time0bt, tce_depth, tce_duration, tce_max_mult_ev, tce_ntoi, tce_bin_oedp_stat, tce_albedo_stat, boot_fap, tce_cap_stat, tce_hap_stat, tce_dicco_msky, tce_ditco_msky, …`. FFI targets: TESS-SPOC HLSP (https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/tess-spoc) runs planet searches on T ≤ 13.5 (plus H ≤ 10, d < 100 pc) FFI targets since May 2021; DV products exist for TCE hosts from Sector 36 (multi-sector from Sector 56) **[verified]** — this is why Chopra's "never searched by SPOC" claim was false.

**DAVE** (Kostov et al. 2019, arXiv:1901.07459) **[verified PDF]**: 772 K2 candidates → 676 PC / 96 FP (49 secondary eclipse, 25 photocentre shift, 17 out-of-transit modulation, 8 non-transit-like, 6 odd/even, 6 V-shaped; 9 with multiple indicators; 60 new FPs). ## 3. Pixel-level and centroid vetting

### 3.1 Difference imaging (Bryson et al. 2013; Twicken et al. 2018; Guerrero 2021)

Per quarter/sector: mean in-transit image minus mean out-of-transit image (balanced before/after windows, avoiding other TCEs and anomalies) = an image of *what dimmed*. Fit the PRF to the difference image and to the out-of-transit image; the offset locates the source. Kepler: 3σ radius of confusion with 0.2" floor; FP if 3σ > 2" or 4σ > 1" (§1.3). TESS SPOC: uncertainty floor ≈ 7.5" (offsets much smaller are never 3σ-significant) **[verified]**. Kostov et al. 2025 (arXiv:2506.05631; TESS Sectors 1-82; 10,001 EBs + ~900,000 ML candidates with score > 0.9) **[verified HTML]**: fit "the TESS Pixel Response Function and a Gaussian Point Spread Function, and adopting the average of the two as the corresponding photocenter"; "photocenter offset threshold of 0.2 pixels (≈4 arcsec)". LEO-Vetter: fail at > 15", trust only prfFitQuality ≥ 0.3. eumemic's calibrated variant **[verified validation/centroid_sectors.md]**: PRF fit per sector (≤ 12), F-test of mean offset vs target and every TIC star within 2' able to produce the depth, 1" per-axis scatter floor, offsets > 60" treated as failed fits, ≥ 2 sectors required, reject at p < 0.001 (flag at 0.001-0.01): 0/565 known planets rejected (3 at p < 0.01), 127/175 (73 %) neighbour-caused signals rejected (81 % at p < 0.01); by neighbour separation: 38 % rejected within 15", 77 % at 15-30", 91 % at 30-60". Flux-only complements: **depth vs aperture radius** (flat for on-target, growing for a neighbour; planethunt:evidence/tess/vetting_plan-18fa5da8.md step 5) and the **ghost/halo diagnostic**.

### 3.4 The pitfall Chopra hit, and the fix **[verified GUIDE.md]**

The first pixel test "could 'locate' a brightening as easily as a dimming"; a "centroid shift" in detector coordinates "disappears once spacecraft rotation is accounted for". Fix: (1) sign-aware fits (fit for a *decrease* consistent with the depth at each candidate source; report the inverted-light-curve fit as a control); (2) joint fit across sectors with each sector's PRF/orientation; (3) balanced before/after out-of-transit frames; (4) test every Gaia/TIC neighbour as the hypothesised source with TESS-band fluxes; (5) work in sky coordinates. Robovetter's CENT_INVERT_DIFF is the same idea.

## 6. Statistical validation

### 6.1 TRICERATOPS (Giacalone et al. 2021, AJ 161, 24; arXiv:2002.00691; https://github.com/stevengiacalone/triceratops)

- **Scenarios**: TP, EB, EBx2P; PTP, PEB, PEBx2P (target is primary of an unresolved bound binary); STP, SEB, SEBx2P (on the unresolved secondary); DTP, DEB, DEBx2P (diluted by an unresolved field star); BTP, BEB, BEBx2P (on that field star); per resolved neighbour with relative depth < 1: NTP, NEB, NEBx2P. **FPP = 1 − (P_TP + P_PTP + P_DTP)**; **NFPP = Σ(NTP + NEB + NEBx2P)**; code: `self.FPP = 1-(prob[0]+prob[3]+prob[9])`, `NFPP = sum(prob[15:])` else 0.0 **[run]** — NFPP is 0 whenever no neighbours are supplied (Kuber's caveat). ### 6.5 Gaia as a companion detector

- **RUWE**: > 1.4 = poor single-star fit (Lindegren 2018); Stassun & Torres 2021 (arXiv:2101.03425): "RUWE is nearly perfectly correlated (r²=0.82) with photocenter motions down to ≲0.1 mas" and "entirely within the nominal 'good' RUWE range of 1.0-1.4", so "RUWE values even slightly greater than 1.0 may signify unresolved binaries" **[verified abstract]**; Penoyre et al. 2022 (arXiv:2202.06963) use a locally renormalised LUWE > 2 **[verified]**; Castro-Ginard et al. 2024 (A&A, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202450172) give sky-dependent RUWE thresholds (GaiaUnlimited). Belokurov 2020 (arXiv:2003.05467): RUWE → centroid-wobble amplitude "for periods up to several years" **[verified]**. Treat RUWE < 1.4 as "no evidence", never a veto — and **run it on every star in the aperture**: the 7.1" neighbour's RUWE is 8.73 **[run]**.
- **CMD over-luminosity**: an unresolved equal-brightness companion lifts a star ~0.75 mag above the single-star sequence; compare M_G (Gaia parallax) with an empirical M-dwarf sequence (method of arXiv:2602.20096, 21,864 unresolved low-mass binaries) — directly constrains STP/PTP.
- **NSS and vari tables** joined on source_id for the target and every neighbour in the aperture (§4.1).
- **Gaia epoch RVs** (G_RVS ≲ 12): radial_velocity_error, rv_nb_transits, rv_amplitude_robust, rv_chisq_pvalue, rv_renormalised_gof — irrelevant for a G = 16 M dwarf.

### 6.6 Multiplicity boost

Lissauer et al. 2012 (arXiv:1201.5424) **[run PDF text]**: boost "of ∼25 for a system with one additional candidate and of ∼100 for a system with more than one additional candidate" (alternative estimates 30 and ∼50); Rowe et al. 2014 (arXiv:1402.6534): 851 planets in 340 systems validated "to substantially better than the 99% confidence level" with ~2 unidentified FPs expected **[verified abstract]**; Guerrero 2021: ≈ 20 / ≈ 54 (< 6 R_E), with a crowded-field caution. Applies only if the second signal is itself a clean, independent candidate.

## 7. Candidate vs validated vs confirmed: realistic routes

AJ/ApJ/MNRAS/A&A accept validation papers when FPP is below the tool's threshold *and* standard follow-up exists (Giacalone 2022: FPP < 0.01, NFPP < 10⁻³, 20 runs, imaging to ~5", recon spectroscopy, photometry of stars within 2.5'). Candidates can be published as candidates (RNAAS, AJ candidate papers) but ExoFOP ingests them only post-publication, and RNAAS counts only if the methods were previously published. Kuber's RNAAS was declined the same day; the audit implies the concerns were sample construction, the full set of search outcomes (31 real vs 32 inverted first-screen passes over 1,736 stars — no excess over the null), the TESS non-detection and the Barber 2022 prior-search question — survey-level honesty items (§8), not a vetting failure.

**What a solo person can reach**
- *Candidate*: yes — §1-5 are free archival computation.
- *Statistically validated*: only with FPP < 0.015 after real constraints and a refereed paper. Bright TESS host: Gaia (MOLUSC) + archival/new speckle + a recon spectrum — one collaborator at a 2-4 m facility or TFOP once a TOI. Kuber's star: the STP term blocks validation; only RUWE/MOLUSC, CMD, AO/speckle, multi-band depths or a resolved transit can shrink it.
- *Confirmed*: mass (RV/TTV) — essentially never solo.

**Kp 16.3 Kepler-field M dwarf (KIC 5689121-like)**: TESS cannot test it (2,400 ppm dip → ~987 ppm in a 1.5-px aperture vs 1,294 ppm scatter; Kuber's PRF injection); ground photometry of a 2,000-ppm, 2.3-h event with a 2× brighter neighbour 7" away needs a 1-2 m telescope, red filter, good seeing, both stars measured simultaneously; Gemini speckle marginal (limit r ≈ 18); Keck LGS-AO feasible if a V ≤ 18 tip-tilt star is available (the G 15.5 neighbour is a lead, not proof); recon spectroscopy needs 8-10 m; precise RVs out of reach. Ceiling: *well-vetted candidate*, possibly *validated* if AO + MOLUSC push STP below ~1 % (Kuber's hypothetical Keck curve reached 0.027). Phrase novelty as "not searched by the Kepler pipeline (no TCE/KOI on this KIC; KBONUS background source); Barber et al. 2022 Table 5 flags Kepler light-curve quality Y", not "never searched".
**Tmag 12 TESS M dwarf**: SG1 photometry of ≥ 1,000 ppm routine with 0.4-1 m telescopes; speckle/AO routine (often on ExoFOP if a TOI); recon spectra with 1.5-4 m; precise RVs feasible on CARMENES/MAROON-X/ESPRESSO/HPF/SPIRou-class instruments; validation and confirmation realistic *with* collaborators; without a TOI, publish first.

## 8. The automated checklist for the Claude Code agent (run on every candidate, in order, thresholds frozen before the search)

Each step appends `{test, inputs, statistic, threshold, pass|fail|inconclusive, code_hash, timestamp}` to `vetting/<candidate>/decision_log.jsonl`; nothing deleted; thresholds never edited after the first candidate (Chopra: "post-hoc hold-out adjustments"; Kuber: the 12.5-14.5 d exclusion and the 26.7/15.5 thresholds "cannot be presented as an independent validation").

**Stage 0 — pre-registration.** Freeze: target list hash and ranking rule, detrending method and windows, search band, number of peaks, first-screen thresholds (SDE/SNR with empirical noise, ≥ 3 events in *each* subset, hold-out SNR, odd/even < 3σ, secondary < 3σ, max single-event contribution < 0.5), hold-out rule (Kuber: last max(2, ceil(0.25 N)) usable quarters/sectors; Chopra: hide a season), control runs (inverted f → 2 − f on every star through the *full* pipeline incl. triage; scrambled seasons; injection-recovery on 5-10 %), stopping rule. `PREREG.md` + git tag.

**Stage 1 — detection sanity.** Empirical noise (same box at ≥ 40 random off-transit phases; Chopra: misfires 15-25 % → 5 %); ≥ 3 good events in discovery and ≥ 2 in hold-out; hold-out depth within 2σ at the frozen ephemeris (no re-search); inverted copy does not reach the same SDE; not within 2 % of TESS orbit multiples or Kepler 3-d desat harmonics unless pixel tests pass; events not on quality-flagged cadences.

**Stage 2 — LEO-Vetter flux level (1.2.0; ~2 s).** Detrended + raw curve with catalogue star; pass = no FA/FP flag at defaults (pre-register any change); log all metrics; run inverted, P/2 and 2P as controls; exovetter ModShift/OddEven/Sweet/Lpp as the second implementation.

**Stage 3 — shape and physics.** batman/emcee or TLS fit: Rp/R*, b, a/R*, T14/T23; derived density within 3σ of Gaia/TIC (Kuber: 4.4 (+2.3 −2.8) ρ☉ vs ~5); V-shape Rp/R* + b < 1.04 (flag) / < 1.5 (fail); Rp < 22 R_E (fail), note < 8 R_E for TRICERATOPS thresholds; depth consistent across quarters/sectors/apertures (for KBONUS fit the dilution model with per-quarter FLFRCSAP/CROWDSAP from the LIGHTCURVE_Qn headers); detrending robustness (3 windows × 2 methods, transit masked) — depth change < 1σ.

**Stage 4 — ephemeris match and novelty (`novelty_check.py`).** KOI cumulative, DR25 TCE, TOI (TAP boxes + separation filter), ExoFOP TOI+CTOI CSVs, MAST TESS SPOC TCE CSVs + TESS-SPOC FFI DV, pscomppars, k2pandc, Villanova + VizieR EBs, Kostov 2025 local copy, Gaia vari/NSS, SIMBAD/VizieR within ≥ 10' (1° for Kp < 10 parents) plus same channel/column (RaDex2Pix or headers); Coughlin 2014 σ_P/σ_T for ratios to 50:1; fail on any match (σ_P ≥ 3.5 and σ_T ≥ 2.0) to a *deeper* object meeting a spatial criterion; record "no match as of <date>"; ADS search of all identifiers.

**Stage 5 — pixel level.** TESS: transit-diffImage + LEO pixel vetting per sector; pass if Δθ ≤ 15" with prfFitQuality ≥ 0.3 in ≥ 2 sectors and eumemic's F-test does not reject the target at p < 0.001 while rejecting each capable neighbour; sign-aware control (inverted curve must not localise). Kepler/KBONUS: (i) neighbours' own PSF curves consistent with 0 and inconsistent with the depth they would need; (ii) per-quarter SAP depth follows the dilution model; (iii) TPF flux-weighted centroids (and the TPF owner's MOM_CENTR columns) match the target and reject neighbours at Δχ² > ~25; (iv) depth vs aperture flat. "Inconclusive" is allowed — report it, do not promote.

**Stage 6 — companion and host checks.** Gaia DR3 RUWE, AEN, ipd_frac_multi_peak, non_single_star, NSS and vari rows for the target **and every star in the aperture** (soft flag RUWE > 1.25-1.4 range per Stassun & Torres; hard flag > 1.4 or any NSS/vari_eclipsing_binary row — note KIC 5689116 at RUWE 8.73); CMD over-luminosity; TIC contamination ratio; ExoFOP imaging/spectroscopy/stellar-companion downloads; secondary-eclipse depth limit vs expected companion eclipse.

**Stage 7 — statistical FPP (TRICERATOPS 1.1.0).** Folded, per-event-baselined light curve with `dilution_corrected` set for the flux type; one cadence; real neighbours within 10 px via `calc_depths`; Gaia field population (never rely on TRILEGAL in Oct 2026); contrast curves (ExoFOP or own); MOLUSC `kept.csv` built with `--gaia --ruwe`; N = 1e6, ≥ 10 seeds; mean ± spread of FPP/NFPP, dominant scenario, skipped scenarios, `FPP_degenerate`; thresholds: validated FPP < 0.015 & NFPP < 10⁻³ (only if Rp < 8 R_E, imaging exists); likely planet FPP < 0.5 & NFPP < 10⁻³; else candidate. Never report an FPP computed without the D/B scenarios as complete.

**Stage 8 — survey-level honesty.** Stars searched (not selected), full table of first-screen passes for real *and* inverted curves, scrambled passes, injection-recovery completeness vs depth/period, false-alarm fraction vs SNR from the inverted sample (eumemic over 798,769 inverted stars: 46 % of clean SNR ≥ 9 signals with MES < 10 are false alarms, 16 % at MES 10-12, 2 % above **[verified README]**), every candidate's disposition with the failing test named (Kuber's 31-vs-32 table is the model). State explicitly when the inverted run contains signals of equal strength.

**Stage 9 — reporting.** "Planet candidate" with FPP x ± y; dominant FP scenario and the single observation that would move it; ephemeris with propagated uncertainty σ_T(N) = √(σ_T0² + N²σ_P²) (Kuber's P/T0 errors give 40.3 min at N = 430 cycles, mid-2027 **[run]**) plus barycentric and proper-motion notes; publish code, data hashes and the decision log (Zenodo DOI) before any announcement; re-check TOI/CTOI/KOI/EB tables on release day.

**Blind-analysis practices distilled from Kuber, Chopra and eumemic**: hold-outs chosen before the search and never re-searched; inverted *and* scrambled controls through the full pipeline (Chopra's first inverted test "searched for only one signal and skipped triage"); fixed pre-written thresholds; decision log with code hashes; empirical off-transit noise; injection-recovery on the same detrending; report both versions when a test changes; never rank by detectability alone (Chopra's top never-2-min stars had contamination ratios 68-222); never claim "never searched" without the TCE tables (Kepler DR25 TCE; MAST TESS TCE CSVs; TESS-SPOC FFI DV) and the literature; propagate timing (Chopra's 6.5-min UTC error; QLP S99-104 bug); independent re-derivation by a second agent (Kuber's `independent_check/` found the depth method-dependent, 1,830-2,430 ppm).
