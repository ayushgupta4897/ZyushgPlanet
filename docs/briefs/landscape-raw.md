## 5. Open ground, ranked for a solo developer with Claude Code

1. Kepler Bonus background FGK dwarfs: 27,378 background sources pass Mehta's quality cuts but are not M dwarfs (G-type 17,087, K 8,206, F 733); 1,259 have G < 14, 4,928 have G 14-16. No transit search exists (only rotation periods, Claytor & Tayar 2025). Validation hard (Kepler field ~1 kpc) but ground photometry works for G < 15 and deep transits. Scoop risk low.
2. Kepler Bonus M dwarfs beyond Mehta's cuts: PSFFRAC > 0.3, CROWDSAP > 0.5, >= 6 quarters gives 5,284 background M dwarfs (3,450 more than his 1,836), 5,950 at >= 4 quarters; plus 2,040 M-dwarf Kepler primary targets (659 pass quality) never searched in PSF photometry. Faint (73% G > 18): only > 2 Re detectable; validation very hard. Scoop risk low-moderate.
3. Kepler Bonus KOI neighbours: 27,566 resolved sources within 30 arcsec of 8,073 KOIs, plus the 181,681 PSF-rereduced Kepler targets (new systematics) for additional small planets and long-period transits. Speculative.
4. TESS M dwarfs T 13.5-16 within 100 pc: ~104k Gaia M dwarfs at G 14-16.5 (20,144 within 60 pc, 83,586 at 60-100 pc). No pipeline light curves below T = 13.5 except T16 (Cycle 1 south) and LEO-Vetter's M-dwarf run. Needs own FFI extraction (TESScut/TGLC/eleanor); detectable > ~2 Re. Validation moderate. Scoop risk moderate.
5. TESS P > 30 d and single/duo transits on FFI-only dwarfs: eumemic capped 4M stars at 30 d; 20-100 d only on 6,932 stars with >= 6 sectors; RAVEN 0.5-16 d; T16 0.5-27 d. Stars with >= 10 sectors (CVZs) at 30-200 d are open; order 10^5 stars (estimate). Validation hard but ephemerides are testable in Year 9. Scoop risk moderate-high (eumemic issue #1).
6. CTOI-only hosts (4,116 TICs, median T 11.6) and 1,397 FP/FA-only TOI hosts: eumemic's TOI-host search excluded both. Masked re-search is cheap with eumemic's open code (~9 s/star). Validation moderate-good (bright, often imaged). Scoop risk high.
7. Newest sectors S105-S108 (S109 FFIs from mid-November): no TESS-SPOC (ends S96) or QLP (ends S104) light curves; SPOC multi-sector TCE search ends S96. Rabtsevich-style "newest sector + archival confirmation" needs custom FFI photometry now. Scoop risk high once QLP catches up.
8. K2: 1,372 open k2pandc candidates, 2,390 confirmed; no AI-agent search found; professional searches stop near Kp 15-16 and short periods; Venner 2026 shows single-transit discoveries are possible. Validation moderate (TESS revisits the ecliptic). Scoop risk low.
9. Clusters (CDIPS/PATHOS) in extended-mission sectors: noisy young stars, low priority for a solo developer.

Closed or crowded: nearby K/M dwarfs T <= 13.5 at 0.5-30 d (eumemic, 97%); 2-min M dwarfs with many sectors (Aidenn8, Comdex4); TOI hosts at 0.5-30 d (eumemic, Tschudi); unpromoted SPOC TCEs (eumemic 14,112, Fraser, Aidenn8); Kepler Bonus M dwarfs under Mehta's exact cuts (1,736 of 1,800).

## 6. Validation reality

Statistical validation needs TRICERATOPS FPP < 0.015 and NFPP < 0.001 plus high-resolution imaging; no AI-agent candidate has reached it, and ExoFOP CTOIs now require a published paper. The working path demonstrated so far is Rabtsevich's: preregistered predictions on Zenodo, a TESS DDT 2-min request (Program 100), and a paper. RNAAS declined Mehta's note; Rabtsevich's remains a Zenodo preprint. Expect candidate-level results and pick regions where an existing archive (Kepler Bonus held-out quarters, K2 + TESS) or the next TESS revisit gives an independent test.
