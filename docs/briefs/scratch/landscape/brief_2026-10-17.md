## 2. Coverage arithmetic
- eumemic has BLS-searched 4,039,203 of the 4,166,638 TIC 8.2 FGKM dwarfs with T<=13.5 (97%) that have any SPOC/TESS-SPOC/QLP light curve, at 0.5-30 d, to SNR 7.5, <=3 signals per star. Gaia DR3 gives ~40k M dwarfs (bp_rp>2) with G<14 within 100 pc (3,184+15,976 within 60 pc; 57+20,696 at 60-100 pc) and ~33k K dwarfs: essentially all of them are in that sample. Fraction of nearby K/M dwarfs with Tmag<13.5 already searched: ~97% (the 3% lack pipeline light curves).
- eumemic's counted candidates are mostly distant (median 262 pc; 43 within 100 pc, 10 within 60 pc) and only 16 have Teff<4000 K: the nearby-M-dwarf prize space has been searched but yielded few counted candidates, so the easy signals there are gone.
- ExoFOP: 8,151 TOIs (330 created in 2026, 3 since 2026-10-09, all SPOC multi-sector); 5,138 CTOIs, 0 new since 2026-10-09 and only one with an October timestamp (PHT, 2026-10-08). The 1,173 CTOIs stamped August 2026 are RAVEN's bulk upload (user lafargamagro). Since ~2026-08-19 ExoFOP accepts only published candidates, so none of the AI-agent candidates can become CTOIs without a paper; Rabtsevich confirmed this in the Reddit thread.
- TESS today: observing Sector 109; S110 starts 2026-10-31. S107 2-min light curves ingested 2026-10-01; TESScut FFIs through S108; S109 orbit-1 FFI early release in progress 2026-10-16. TESS-SPOC FFI light curves stop at S96 (Year 7 bundle); QLP through S104; SPOC multi-sector TCE search through S96.

## 5. Open ground (ranked for a solo developer with Claude Code)
1. Kepler Bonus background FGK dwarfs: 27,378 background sources pass Kuber's quality cuts but are not M dwarfs (G-type 17,087, K 8,206, F 733); 1,259 have G<14 and 4,928 have G 14-16. Nobody has run a transit search on them (only rotation periods: Claytor & Tayar 2025). Validation hard (Kepler field, ~1 kpc) but ground photometry feasible for G<15 and >0.3% depths. Scoop risk low.
2. Kepler Bonus M dwarfs beyond Kuber's cuts: relaxing to PSFFRAC>0.3, CROWDSAP>0.5, >=6 quarters gives 5,284 background M dwarfs (3,450 more than his 1,836), 5,950 at >=4 quarters; plus 2,040 M-dwarf Kepler primary targets (659 pass quality) that have never been searched in PSF photometry. Faint (73% G>18): only >2 Re planets detectable; validation very hard. Scoop risk low-moderate (Mehta could extend).
3. Kepler Bonus KOI neighbours: 27,566 resolved sources within 30" of 8,073 KOIs with PSF light curves; a blended-neighbour planet search has not been published. Also 181,681 Kepler primary targets re-reduced with PSF photometry (new systematics): a KBonus-based re-search of Kepler M dwarfs and KOI hosts for additional small planets. Speculative.
4. TESS faint M dwarfs T 13.5-16 within 100 pc: ~104k Gaia M dwarfs at G 14-16.5 (20,144 within 60 pc, 83,586 at 60-100 pc). No SPOC/TESS-SPOC/QLP light curves below T=13.5 (QLP) except T16 (Cycle 1 south, 0.5-27 d) and whatever LEO-Vetter's 200k M-dwarf run covered. Needs own FFI extraction (TESScut/TGLC/eleanor). Detectable: >~2 Re with multi-sector stacking. Validation moderate (deep transits, ground photometry). Scoop risk moderate (Kunimoto, T16 extensions).
5. TESS periods >30 d and single/duo transits on FFI-only dwarfs: eumemic capped the 4M-star search at 30 d and searched 20-100 d only on 6,932 stars with >=6 sectors; RAVEN 0.5-16 d; T16 0.5-27 d. Stars T<=13.5 with >=10 sectors (CVZs) at P 30-200 d are open. Star count O(10^5) (estimate). Validation hard (few transits) but high value. Scoop risk moderate (eumemic issue #1 proposes exactly this).
6. CTOI-only hosts: 4,116 TICs have CTOIs but no TOI (RAVEN 1,148, DIAmante 921, DTARPS 198, ...), median T=11.6; eumemic's TOI-host search covered only the 6,430 CP/KP/PC/APC TOI hosts, and 1,397 TOI hosts whose TOIs are all FP/FA were excluded too. Masking the known signal and searching for additional planets is cheap (eumemic's code). Validation moderate (bright stars). Scoop risk high (eumemic could add it in a day).
7. Newest sectors S105-S108 (and S109 early-release FFIs): no TESS-SPOC (ends S96) and no QLP (ends S104) light curves exist for S105+; eumemic's K/M sample used QLP S97-105 lists only. A Rabtsevich-style "detect in the newest sector, confirm archivally" search needs custom FFI photometry for S105-S108 now and S109 from mid-November. Scoop risk high for bright stars once QLP catches up.
8. K2: 1,372 k2pandc candidates, 2,390 confirmed; no AI-agent search found; professional searches (Kruse 2019, Zink 2021, TFAW C1-8/C12-18, K2-OjOS) stop at Kp~15-16 and short periods; single-transit search (Venner 2026 found HD 137010 b). Open: faint K2 M dwarfs, long-period/single transits, campaigns 16-19, no PSF background product exists (speculative gap). Validation moderate (ecliptic fields get TESS revisits). Scoop risk low.
9. Clusters (CDIPS/PATHOS): newer sectors with PSF photometry of young stars; noisy, hard for a solo dev; low priority.

Closed or crowded: nearby K/M dwarfs T<=13.5 at 0.5-30 d (eumemic, 97% searched); 2-min M dwarfs with many sectors (Aidenn8, Comdex4); TOI hosts 0.5-30 d (eumemic, Tschudi); unpromoted SPOC TCEs (eumemic 14,112; Fraser; Aidenn8); Kepler Bonus M dwarfs with Kuber's exact cuts (1,736 of 1,800).

## 6. Validation reality for a solo developer
Statistical validation needs TRICERATOPS FPP<0.015 and NFPP<0.001 plus high-resolution imaging; no AI-agent candidate has reached it. The workable path demonstrated so far: preregistered prediction (Zenodo) + TESS DDT 2-min request (Rabtsevich, Program 100) + a paper (RNAAS has declined Mehta's; Rabtsevich's is a Zenodo preprint). ExoFOP CTOIs now require a published paper. Expect candidate-level results; choose regions where the next TESS revisit or an existing archive (Kepler Bonus seasons, K2+TESS) gives an independent test.
