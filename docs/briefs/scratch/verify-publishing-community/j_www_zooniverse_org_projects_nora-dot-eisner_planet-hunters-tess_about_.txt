Confirmation of a Transiting Giant Planet on an Eccentric 261 Day Orbit with the Automated Planet Finder Telescope: here TOI-2180 b (TIC 298663873) This planet was detected as a single transit in sector 19 by PHT volunteers and registered as a PHT cTOI. It was simultaneously detected by another citizen science project. A team led by Paul A. Dalba from the Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics, University of California, Santa Cruz carried out further observations and analysis and characterised the planet as a Jupiter sized planet with nearly three times Jupiter’s mass on an eccentric 261 day orbit around a slightly evolved mid-G-type star. The PHT volunteers who are now co-authors on this paper are: Hans Martin Schwengeler and Ivan Terentev.
