Dear Editor,

Please consider the attached Research Note, "Three Sub-Neptune Transit Candidates Around Kepler Bonus Background Stars".

The note reports three transit candidates (KIC 6699887, KIC 3831969 and KIC 11138423) from a pre-registered search of 6,465 Kepler Bonus background dwarfs that were never Kepler targets. Each signal localizes on the target star in Kepler difference images, has no ephemeris match to any known Kepler object, and gives a transit-derived stellar density consistent with Gaia DR3. With TRICERATOPS and Gaia-based companion constraints (MOLUSC), their false-positive probabilities are 0.6 to 0.7 percent, with zero nearby-star false-positive probability. The note presents them as candidates and states that high-resolution imaging is needed before validation.

The search used thresholds frozen before the search, an inverted-flux null on every star, and positive controls. Revisions made after early results are dated in a public log, and the note labels the affected results as post hoc. All code, run outputs, light curves and evidence packs are archived at [Zenodo DOI].
