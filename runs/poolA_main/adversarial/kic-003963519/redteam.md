## 1. What I tried to kill it with, and what happened

### 1.1 Blended / background eclipsing binary
- **Pixel localisation, my own method** (season-stacked, event-differenced deficit maps; empirical PRF template = the G=14.86 TPF host shifted to each trial position; amplitude fitted by weighted least squares; grid extended 3 px beyond the stamp). Delta-chi2 vs null, summed over the four seasons (137 stacked events):
  - target (G 17.28): **70.1**; implied depth on the target from the pixel amplitude, scaling the target flux from the host by Delta-G: **1053 +- 135 ppm** -- the same depth as the light curve (1061-1194 ppm). Per season: ch36 (Q3,7,11,15) dchi2 16.9, 900 +- 219 ppm, best-fit position 1.4" from the target; ch80 (Q4,8,12,16) dchi2 48.0, 1540 +- 222 ppm, best fit 0.5" from the target; ch52 (Q1,5,9,17) dchi2 2.5, 457 +- 290 ppm (weak, best fit 4.6" away but target inside the 3-sigma region); ch8 (Q2 only, 12 events) dchi2 2.6, 1348 +- 829 ppm. Target inside the 1-sigma position region in 3/4 seasons, inside 3-sigma in 4/4.
  - best alternatives: TPF host KIC 3963530 (G 14.86, 10.4") dchi2 11.4, implied 68 +- 32 ppm; Gaia ...355069184 (G 20.27, 8.8") dchi2 11.3, implied 6054 +- 2176 ppm; ...520062387200 (G 18.67, 7.0", the nearest neighbour) **dchi2 2.0**, implied 140 +- 363 ppm; ...520062393600 (G 16.94, 15") dchi2 4.2.

Gaia DR3 to G ~ 20.9 shows no source off the edge within 3 px of the target along the edge normal; the nearest off-stamp source (...355060480, G 20.63) is 2.8 px away along the edge and lies outside the 1-sigma regions (y 2.75-3.0 px vs 5.2). The bright G = 12.5 star 42" away (...421286411904) is 9-10 px beyond the edge, has no DR25 TCE (none within 3 arcmin), and its PRF wing there is negligible. An unresolved companion within ~1-2" of the target is **not** excluded by any pixel test and is the job of TRICERATOPS.
- **Shape / secondary / odd-even (box_depth = in-transit minus 1-3 dur sidebands; error = std of 500 random-phase boxes):** trapezoid fit T14 2.37 h, T23/T14 = 0.97 (flat-bottomed); a forced V shape is worse by delta-chi2 = 54 (chi2 36323 vs 36269 on 5360 points); odd 1226 +- 172 vs even 1164 +- 190 ppm (0.24 sigma); phase-0.5 box 59 ppm (3x-duration box 66 ppm) against a 124 ppm random-phase error; strongest dip anywhere in phase 0.05-0.95 is 377 ppm at phase 0.89 (3.0 sigma, post-hoc over 181 phases; the strongest bump is -367 ppm, i.e. symmetric noise).

### 1.2 Stellar variability / rotation aliases
- Lomb-Scargle on raw per-quarter-normalised PSF flux: top peak 52.5 d (power 0.057, long-term systematics); power at P = 9e-6, at P/2 similar.
- On the 1-d-detrended flux (0.3-3 d): **0.62027 d, half-amplitude 100 ppm**, power 7e-4. This is the periodicity the pipeline's inverted null and "second signal" found at stage 1 (inverted: 0.619 d, SDE 12.9, SNR 8.1, failed only on duty cycle; real second signal 0.6203 d, SDE 18.2) -- a low-amplitude sinusoid present in both polarities, i.e. real variability (rotation or a blended short-period variable). P/P_LS = 10.99, so 11 cycles per transit period; the sinusoid cannot build a coherent 2.4-h box (its phase drifts 2.4 cycles over the baseline), and after subtracting the fitted sinusoid the box depth is **1177 +- 146 ppm**. ls_amp/depth = 0.09.

## 2. What survives
1. Blind recovery by independent code at the top of a 1-50 d search (re-derivation SDE 21.7 vs inverted-null 8.4); pipeline stage-1 real pass with the inverted null failing (inverted best 0.619 d, not P).
2. Depth consistent across four flux products, four channels, 14 quarters (chi2 10.1/13), 148 events, after removing the 10 strongest events, and far from gaps.
3. Flat-bottomed, no odd/even difference, no secondary at any phase above 3 sigma post-hoc.
4. Pixel deficit localised at the target by two independent methods; pixel-implied depth on the target (1053 +- 135 ppm) equals the light-curve depth; the nearest neighbour (7") is excluded as the source (dchi2 2 vs 70); the pre-registered v16 chi2 margins are met with my analysis (59) and the pipeline's (84).
5. No known object, no ephemeris match anywhere in the field, host never a Kepler target.

## 3. Residual risks (why this is "survives", not "validated")
- **Unresolved companion / hierarchical EB within ~1-2"**: untestable with Kepler pixels; needs TRICERATOPS with the Gaia neighbour list and, ideally, high-resolution imaging. Stage-1 inverted-null statistics do not help here.
- **Stamp-edge truncation**: 30-40% of the target PRF is off silicon; localisation perpendicular to the edge is weaker than it looks and the ch52 season is inconclusive on its own.
- **Modest SNR** (8.5-9.6 empirical) and a 1.5-sigma depth deficit in year 3 / the ch52 season.
- **Host radius uncertain** (parallax SNR 3.8; GSP-Phot 0.55-0.74 Rsun vs pipeline 0.77): implied radius 2-3 Re, up to ~7 Re if the star is evolved. Needs a spectrum or at least an extinction-corrected CMD argument before any radius is quoted.
- The 0.62-d, 100-ppm sinusoid should be identified (rotation of the K dwarf vs a blended variable) because it bears on the host characterisation.
