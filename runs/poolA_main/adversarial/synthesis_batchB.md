## Caveats that travel with every object

- **Post-hoc rules.** Stage-2 v1.5-v1.7 and the v1.6 pixel chi2 comparison were registered after results had been seen. Every stage-2 and pixel verdict above is post-hoc relative to the stage-1 registration and must be labelled as such in any write-up.
- **Pixel localisation of edge stars and sub-pixel blends.** Background sources sit at stamp edges by construction; the v1.5 centroid verdict failed its positive control and is descriptive only. Neither the pipeline's Gaussian (sigma 0.9 px) model nor the red teams' Gaussian/empirical-template models is a Kepler PRF; a PRF forward model is on every live object's list. Kepler pixels cannot exclude an EB within ~1-3 arcsec for any object: that is what a documented TRICERATOPS run plus a contrast curve must price before the word "candidate" is used in public.
- **KBONUS fractional depths are not on-target depths.** Three of seven objects (kic-011138423, kic-010213563, kic-006367225) show season- or module-dependent PSF depth driven by how much flux the KBONUS PSF model assigns to the target per stamp geometry. Quote absolute lost light (e/s) alongside any ppm depth, and treat a season dependence as a blend/dilution statistic.
- **Ephemeris matching must be channel-wide.** kic-011285493 shows that a bright EB 5.6 arcmin away on the same channel leaks ~2 e/s into unrelated KBONUS light curves through the extraction itself; the 3-arcmin cone cannot catch this. Until the match is extended (harmonics 1/3..3, |dP/P| < 1e-3, epoch within one duration, EB-catalogue periods for koi_fpflag_ss = 1 objects) and the TPF host's KBONUS flux is compared with its official SAP/PDCSAP, every surviving signal carries a residual risk of the same mechanism. kic-011285117 (stage-1 survivor at 39.89 d) is already known to carry that EB's clock at 6.2 sigma.
- **Unmodelled Gaia sources inside the PSF.** kic-010213563 (G 18.35 at 0.97 arcsec) and kic-006367225 (G 20.49 at 3.6 arcsec) were both undone by Gaia sources absent from the KBONUS source catalogue. A pre-stage-2 flag for Gaia sources within ~1.5 arcsec not in the KBONUS model is recommended.
- **Host parameters at kpc distances.** GSP-Phot radii in the targets file can be inconsistent with the parallax distance (kic-011285493: 0.87 Rsun at 1948 pc vs M_G 4.56 at 2858 pc -> 1.3-1.6 Rsun; kic-003963519: parallax SNR 3.8). Planet radii and t14_max derived from `rstar_used` are lower bounds until the host is characterised.
- **Novelty is dated.** "Not in KOI / DR25 TCE / Kepler EB / confirmed / TOI / Bienias & Szabo 2025 / Moquin 2026 / SIMBAD" holds for the catalogues in `data/known/` fetched 2026-10-17T15:46:52Z; the Kepler Bonus team's own list is unpublished, so the correct statement is "not in any public list as of 2026-10-17".
- **Which statistic.** Where pipeline and re-derivation SNRs differ, the depths agree and the difference is the noise estimator (pipeline `box_noise_ppm` vs random-phase box scatter). Quote the more conservative value and name it.
